# Classification of Document Types in UiPath Document Understanding

## Lesson Objectives
* Categorize documents into **Structured**, **Semi-Structured**, and **Unstructured** types.
* Understand data extraction strategies and model selection based on document layout variability.

---

## Document Taxonomy & Characteristics

### 1. Structured Documents
* **Layout Characteristics**: Fixed, rigid layout where fields, tables, and checkboxes maintain identical positions across all document instances.
* **Extraction Complexity**: Low complexity; predictable pixel/coordinate locations allow rule-based template anchors.
* **Common Examples**:
  * Government Tax Forms (e.g., W-2, 1040)
  * Standardized Surveys & Questionnaires
  * Passports & Driver's Licenses
  * Printed Timesheets

### 2. Semi-Structured Documents
* **Layout Characteristics**: Shared data fields and key-value pair patterns across instances, but layout structure, positioning, and table lengths vary dynamically between vendors or templates.
* **Extraction Complexity**: Medium complexity; requires pre-trained Machine Learning (ML) models or hybrid intelligent keywords/Regex patterns rather than static positional anchors.
* **Common Examples**:
  * Vendor Invoices
  * Sales & OCR Receipts
  * Purchase Orders (POs)
  * Utility & Bank Statements

### 3. Unstructured Documents
* **Layout Characteristics**: Free-form running text with no fixed structure, predictable key-value positioning, or uniform tabular layout.
* **Extraction Complexity**: High complexity; requires Natural Language Processing (NLP), Named Entity Recognition (NER), or Generative AI / Large Language Models (LLMs).
* **Common Examples**:
  * Legal Contracts & Leases
  * Service Level Agreements (SLAs)
  * Annual Financial Reports & Disclosures
  * Corporate News & Communication

---

## Comparative Matrix

| Category | Layout Variability | Extraction Method in UiPath | Model Complexity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Structured** | None (Fixed layout) | Form Extractor / Template Matching | Low |
| **Semi-Structured** | Partial (Variable layout, consistent fields) | ML Extractor / Generative Extractor | Medium |
| **Unstructured** | High (Free-form prose text) | Generative / NLP Extractor | High |

---

## Technical Strategy in UiPath Document Understanding
While rule-based tools (Form Extractor, Regex Extractor) work efficiently for structured documents, semi-structured and unstructured variants require **UiPath Document Understanding** leveraging Machine Learning or Generative Extractor endpoints to ensure resilient, vendor-agnostic data extraction.

---

## Conclusion
Identifying document structure is the foundation of Document Understanding architecture. Matching the document type to the appropriate extractor mechanism maximizes extraction accuracy and reduces human-in-the-loop validation tasks in UiPath Action Center.
