Once the portal is launched:

- Navigate to _Storage accounts_
- Open the pre-existing storage account named `storage...`
- 
![[Pasted image 20260602201719.png]]

Setting up a Storage account comes with a variety of configuration options. Let’s take a closer look at some key settings:

On the _Overview_ page of the Storage account, take a note of the following:

- _Performance_
- _Location_
- _Replication_

---

**Performance**
Standard
**Location**
eastus
**Replication**
Locally redundant storage (LRS)

---

Let’s take a look at the different ways we can store data in a Storage account.

- Using the menu on the left, navigate to _Data storage_
- In this drop-down, you’ll see several storage options available, take a moment to note the different types of data storage options.
- Click on _Containers_ — we’ll be using this shortly to store our data.

At Cipher Coffee, your team needs a place to store everything from product images to order receipts. Containers will help them keep things organized, just like shelves in a well stocked café



#### In the Storage account overview page what is the "Replication" set to?

A/ Locally-redundant storage (LRS)
