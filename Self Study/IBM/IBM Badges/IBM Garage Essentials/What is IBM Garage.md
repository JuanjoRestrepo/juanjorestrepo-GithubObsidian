IBM Garage is an expert methodology to accelerate business transformation. 

It provokes imaginations, encourages the taking of manageable risks, helps teams get creative, and provides the tools, technologies, and expertise to streamline product development.

![[Pasted image 20240624204855.png]]


The goal is to **quickly deploy and scale maximum-impact solutions across the enterprise** – and to foster a faster, more sustainable approach to innovation.

### Why did IBM develop the Garage approach? What specific problems is it looking to solve?
---
Garage exists to respond to the challenges enterprise leaders face.

In the current market environment, enterprises are consistently seeking improvements and competitive advantages in three key areas

#### 1. Innovation
It drives real value and shows tangible business results.

Enterprises continually explore new ways to respond to change and accelerate speed to market. Whether they're looking to disrupt or they themselves are being disrupted by new conditions, enterprise leaders across IT and business lines want to prioritize their investments with confidence and minimize risk.

#### 2. Application of leading technologies to digital transformations

The utility of any technology, even leading ones like hybrid cloud, AI, or blockchain, is dependent on how it is architected, implemented, and integrated.
Business leaders need a mechanism to continually experiment with new technology options while managing legacy decisions

#### 3. Culture

More and more, businesses understand that culture matters. They want to create empowered, high-performing teams that are ready to integrate modern methods like lean, design thinking, agile, and DevOps into real, enterprise delivery situations.

Many enterprises apply Agile techniques today but want to push their product management practice even further to become more product and user-centric across IT and Business.

### What do enterprises need to tackle these challenges?

#### 1. Culture: 
Teams at enterprise scale companies, and even occasionally at IBM, struggle with outdated and limiting processes and ways of working. These legacy approaches limit creativity, productivity, and potential impact. 

To develop a culture of innovation, companies need **space:** space to work in new ways, to experiment and learn, and to apply new approaches to problem solving. 

The special alchemy of a Garage in full flow, iteratively delivering outcomes at a cadence that legacy teams struggle to match, is a key element in demonstrating how an innovative culture can and should work.

![[Pasted image 20240624205932.png]]

#### 2. Expertise: 

The way a new technology is architected and implemented is just as important as the technology itself. 21st century winners are defined by how quickly and effectively they can form teams around important problems. 

IBM Garage Squads are **multidisciplinary, collaborative teams** focused on building the right thing at startup speed. 

Squads allows Garages to collaborate across disciplines and silos, providing direct avenues for IBM’s domain **experts, designers, and developers to implement correctly at speed.**
