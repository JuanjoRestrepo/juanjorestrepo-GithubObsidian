### Enterprise Design Thinking

![[Pasted image 20240624212027.png]]

It helps us focus on our users, align our teams around their needs, and decide what product will be most impactful to build for them. 

Agile development principles help us build that product fast and in the right way, using agile ceremonies, pair programming, and test-driven development to deliver quality software, quickly.

![[Pasted image 20240624212118.png]]

Lean Startup helps bring rigor and measurement to our process. Applying an experimental approach with careful testing of hypotheses helps us confirm that we’re moving in the right direction while accurately measuring our impact along the way.

![[Pasted image 20240624212148.png]]

DevOps makes sure we’re working efficiently and preparing for scale. Automation of delivery and integration means our code is ready to grow, if and when needed.

![[Pasted image 20240624212154.png]]


The value of IBM Garage is the way it brings these practices together and orchestrates them to deliver value for the enterprise.


### The Garage Phases: 
Garage is generally divided into 3 distinct phases.

![[Pasted image 20240624212255.png]]

The first phase involves prioritizing and developing a vision through Co-creation, where we work together to imagine a new future and validate that vision with our end users

![[Pasted image 20240624212339.png]]

The second phase involves proving out and deploying our ideas to market with Co-execution, where we iteratively build MVPs and measure how they meet user needs and deliver business outcomes.

The third phase involves hardening and scaling our solution over time while driving continued transformation with Co-operation, where we expand our impact by bringing new capabilities to our end users and to our organizations.

![[Pasted image 20240624212522.png]]


Whether we're building a new app or modernizing old workloads, we apply this consistent, iterative approach to imagine new solutions, pivot with confidence, and harden for the enterprise.
![[Pasted image 20240624212543.png]]

The key principles of a successful IBM Garage are:

- Focus on our end user to design and deliver solutions that delight them and that deliver business outcomes. 
- Rapidly and progressively deliver business value against our end user’s goals and needs.
- Leverage evidence-based decision making to inform how you deliver as well as how you work throughout the lifecycle. 
- Build the highest level of quality that you can to meet the requirements that you have. Don’t over-plan and don’t anticipate.
- Small projects through scaled, enterprise projects follow the same lifecycle approach from idea to production, production to learning, and learning to improvement. 
- Start with a proven and opinionated set of practices and adapt them to your specific culture and situation.
- Delivering great outcomes requires diverse, empowered teams that respect one another.


### The Squad:
---
![[Pasted image 20240624212829.png]]

A Squad is a multi-disciplinary team - researchers, developers, designers, business strategists, and data scientists - using the Garage methodology to realize value.

An effective squad is at the heart of a successful Garage, and is a fundamentally collaborative effort. The best squads are a seamless blend of IBMers, our clients, and even third-party contributors.

![[Pasted image 20240624212846.png]]
![[Pasted image 20240624212851.png]]

Squads can vary in size and skills depending on the challenge in front of them. Some Garage challenges might require more technical skills, while others might need specific design or industry expertise. Some roles, though, are consistent. A squad lead from IBM and a product owner from the client should be present in every squad.
![[Pasted image 20240624212905.png]]

Garages prefer squad members with T-shaped skills - a deep expertise in one area combined with a broad base of understanding in others - for their flexibility and ability to bring diverse perspectives.

### The Culture of a Squad
---
The culture of a squad is as important to the success of a Garage as the architecture or the user experience of the solutions it delivers.

![[Pasted image 20240624213045.png]]

Squads are diverse, empowered teams. Their way of working is a fundamental example to the rest of the organization of how impactful, motivational, and fun it can be to work in an IBM Garage

![[Pasted image 20240624213052.png]]

We want to highlight some specific cultural practices that we believe are instrumental to the success of an IBM Garage:

1. **User and Value Focus:** As squads, your focus and empathy should always be on the user of the product being developed.
2. **Agility and Continuous Improvement:** Garage organizations deliver more consistently because they create a culture where **failure is embraced instead of feared.** that is viewed as a positive, and as an opportunity to learn and pivot towards a better outcome
3. **Safety and Fun:** They are also human beings, with the same emotions, challenges, and struggles as everyone else. Creating a safe, fun work culture is vital for squad members to feel engaged and empowered to deliver their best work.
