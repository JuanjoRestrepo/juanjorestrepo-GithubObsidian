
## Lesson Objectives
* Master the steps required to configure a time trigger in UiPath Orchestrator for automated, scheduled job execution.
* Gain a clear understanding of the various trigger types available within the UiPath ecosystem.

## Step-by-Step Configuration Guide

### 1. Access UiPath Orchestrator
* Open your web browser and log in to your **UiPath Orchestrator** tenant.

### 2. Navigate to Triggers
* Select the target folder from the left-hand navigation pane and open the **Triggers** section.

### 3. Initialize a New Trigger
* Click the **Add Trigger** button located on the top action toolbar.

### 4. Select the Target Process
* Choose the specific published process you wish to automate from the **Process** drop-down menu.

### 5. Configure Schedule & Recurrence
* Set up the time-based parameters:
  * **Start Date and Time**: Specify the precise timestamp for initial execution.
  * **Recurrence Pattern**: Select the scheduling frequency (e.g., Minutely, Hourly, Daily, Weekly, or custom CRON expressions).
  * **Time Zone**: Ensure the target execution timezone aligns with system requirements.

### 6. Save & Activate
* Review the trigger parameters and click **Save** to enable the schedule.

### 7. Monitor Scheduled Runs
* Track upcoming and executed instances under the **Jobs** and **Triggers** views to verify consistent operational execution.

## Conclusion
Time triggers remove the need for manual dispatch by running processes on a pre-established cadence. Following these steps enables predictable, hands-off execution of your unattended workflows.
