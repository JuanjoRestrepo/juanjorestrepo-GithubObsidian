# What is Data Science?

Data Science is about extracting knowledge and insights from data, and finding unseen patterns, deriving meaningful information and making business decisions

# What is the purpose of Data Science?

- Create strategies for a business to make better decisions
- Using data to create as much impact as possible for your company

# What is the Data Science process?

- Data collection
- Transform and clean data
- Explore, analyze and visualize data
- Use Machine Learning models
